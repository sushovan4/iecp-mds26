The Intersection Euler Characteristic Profile

Euler Calculus, Stability, and Certified Topological Interaction of Point Clouds

Sushovan Majhi

16 November 2026

How much do they interact?

How much do several point clouds interact topologically, and at what scale?

Far apart. X’s barcode ignores Y; that of X\cup Y gains a bar dying at half their distance. The overlap stays empty until the balls meet.

Touching. The overlap is a lens: \Delta\chi=1.

Inside the hole. X\cup Y’s barcode changes X’s own loop (it dies at 0.55, not 1.40) and gains the gap’s. The overlap is the meeting.

§ I. Euler Calculus

The profile

arXiv:2608.06180

For pairwise disjoint finite clouds \mathcal X=(X_1,\dots,X_k) in \mathbb R^d, with \mathcal U(X;t)=\bigcup_{p\in X}B(p,t), and scales \mathbf t\in\mathbb R^k_{\ge0}, \Delta\chi(\mathbf t;\mathcal X)\;=\;\int_{\mathbb R^d}\prod_{i=1}^k \mathbf 1_{\mathcal U(X_i;t_i)}\,d\chi .

\Delta\chi(\mathbf t;\mathcal X)\;=\;\chi\Big(\bigcap_{i=1}^k\mathcal U(X_i;t_i)\Big). On the diagonal, for two clouds, \Delta\chi(r)=\chi_X(r)+\chi_Y(r)-\chi_{X\cup Y}(r).

An integer-valued curve, not a diagram: it enters a test or a learning pipeline as it is, with no vectorization.

Three configurations

arXiv:2608.06180

At the marked scale the overlap is empty, a single region, and an annulus: \Delta\chi=0,\ 1,\ 0. The encircling clouds meet, yet \chi of an annulus is 0.

Why not persistence of the overlap?

arXiv:2608.06180

For A=\mathcal U(X;t), B=\mathcal U(Y;s) and \Delta\beta_q=\beta_q(A)+\beta_q(B)-\beta_q(A\cup B), \beta_q(A\cap B)=\Delta\beta_q+\delta_q+\delta_{q+1}, with \delta_q the rank of the connecting map H_q(A\cup B)\to H_{q-1}(A\cap B). The alternating sum cancels the \delta’s: \chi(A\cap B)=\chi(A)+\chi(B)-\chi(A\cup B).

The nerve of the cross-cloud lenses B(x,t)\cap B(y,s) computes the overlap. It has up to mn vertices, and \ell lenses with a common point span 2^\ell-1 faces, with \ell as large as mn.

\Delta\chi is the Euler characteristic curve of the overlap filtration, the alternating sum of its Betti curves, read off the Alpha complexes of the clouds and of their union: cheap enough for 999 permutations per test. The persistence of the overlap is stable too (Theorem 4), and the paper refines \chi by a relative module.

On an annulus \beta_0=\beta_1=1 and \chi=0: the encircling clouds. Hence the guard of § III.

Why this curve

arXiv:2608.06180

Let \Phi assign to each \mathcal X an integer profile, with

  • pointwise Euler form: \Phi(\mathcal X)(\mathbf t)=\int\varphi\big(\mathbf 1_{\mathcal U(X_1;t_1)},\dots,\mathbf 1_{\mathcal U(X_k;t_k)}\big)\,d\chi for some \varphi:\mathbb Z^k\to\mathbb Z, \varphi(\mathbf 0)=0;
  • separation: \Phi(\mathcal X)(\mathbf t)=0 when the k-fold overlap is empty;
  • normalization: \Phi(\mathcal X)(\mathbf t)=1 when the overlap is a single lens \bigcap_i B(x_i,t_i).

Then \Phi=\Delta\chi.

It decides which Euler-level summary of the overlap, not that its value measures strength: \chi is signed and not monotone. A stronger form asks only for a valuation in each cloud that depends on the overlap’s homotopy type, with separation and normalization.

§ II. Computation and Stability

Computation

arXiv:2608.06180

For |X|+|Y|=n in fixed dimension d, sweeping the Alpha complexes of X, Y and X\cup Y in birth order computes \Delta\chi in O\big(n^{\lceil d/2\rceil}\log n\big) time and O\big(n^{\lceil d/2\rceil}\big) space, forming no boundary matrix.

Each Euler characteristic curve is a running sum \sum_{a(\sigma)\le r}(-1)^{\dim\sigma} over simplices sorted by birth. No persistence reduction.

In even d the exponent is optimal for any algorithm that builds the filtration. k clouds by inclusion–exclusion, O\big(2^k n^{\lceil d/2\rceil}\log n\big); off the diagonal, a weighted Alpha complex.

Stability

arXiv:2608.06180

Let \epsilon=\max_i d_H(X_i,X_i'). The overlap filtrations of \mathcal X and \mathcal X' are \epsilon-interleaved; \Delta\chi(r)=\Delta\chi'(r) whenever [r-\epsilon,r+\epsilon] holds no critical scale of either; and \|\Delta\chi-\Delta\chi'\|_{L^1}\le2\epsilon\,(N+N'), with N,N' the numbers of bars.

\Delta\chi itself admits no interleaving distance, and no L^1 bound free of the bar count can hold.

Consistency

arXiv:2608.06180

Let A_1,\dots,A_k\subset\mathbb R^d be compact with reach at least \tau>0, sampled i.i.d. from measures supported on them and positive on balls; let \rho\mapsto T_\rho=\bigcap_i\mathcal U(A_i;\rho) have finitely many critical values in (0,\tau), and r\in(0,\tau) be regular for it. Then almost surely, for all large samples, the \epsilon_{\max}-persistent Euler characteristic of the sample overlap equals \chi(T_r), where \epsilon_{\max}=\max_i d_H(X_i,A_i)\to0; and if r is also regular for the sample, \Delta\chi(r;X_1,\dots,X_k)=\chi(T_r) exactly.

O\big(\epsilon^{-k_i}(\log\tfrac1\epsilon+\log\tfrac1\delta)\big) points from A_i for confidence 1-\delta, with \epsilon set by the distance from r to the critical scales: the intrinsic dimension k_i, not the ambient d.

§ III. Certified Interaction

Every number carries a test

arXiv:2609.08561

With \Phi(\Delta\chi)=\int|\Delta\chi(r)|\,dr, \widehat E=\frac{\Phi\big(\Delta\chi(\cdot;X_1,\dots,X_k)\big)}{\mathbb E_{H_0}\,\Phi}\,, about 1 when the labels are exchangeable, tending to 0 under separation.

If the labels of the pooled sample are exchangeable, the two one-sided permutation p-values satisfy \Pr[p\le\alpha]\le\alpha for every statistic, every \alpha, every sample size and every number B of permutations.

A separation claim is withheld when \Delta\chi is silent just beyond the first interaction scale \hat r^\ast, where the unions provably meet. Unguarded, the test certifies an annular overlap as separated, with probability tending to one.

Monsoon onset

A four-signal onset detector: the profile between the delay-embedded windows before and after each day, with complexity variance, Higuchi fractal dimension and a rolling mean. Delete the profile, or swap it for a channel that sees no data, and test the paired change in error.

Disentanglement, certified

arXiv:2609.08561

Class-conditional clouds of 111 trained networks: 1{,}170 (network, layer, checkpoint) cells, 52{,}650 certified pairwise measurements.

The mean pairwise quotient falls with depth: 0.72\to0.63\to0.57\to0.18 on ResNet-56, CIFAR-10.

12{,}375 paired sign-flip tests between consecutive checkpoints, on a scale-free statistic: 2{,}707 significant steps, 2{,}026 of them decreases, 1{,}728 within the first 8 epochs.

Mostly pairwise

arXiv:2609.08561

For classes c,c',c'' at layer \ell: \ \rho_\ell=\widehat E_\ell(c,c',c'')\,\big/\,\max\big\{\widehat E_\ell(c,c'),\widehat E_\ell(c,c''),\widehat E_\ell(c',c'')\big\}. Only a k-fold profile can pose it.

\rho\le1 in 1{,}509 of 1{,}560 cells (96.7\%), median 0.63. An unstructured null already reaches 83\%; the evidence is the medians, 0.44–0.89 against 0.85–1.03 (p=3.3\times10^{-8}). A regularity, not a law.

Memorization, counted

arXiv:2610.00251

Does a generator copy its training images? Compare its samples’ overlap with training images T and held-out images H; if it copies nothing, relabelling T and H is an exact null.

T_{\rm small}=\int_0^{r_0}|\Delta\chi(r;G,T)|\,dr-\int_0^{r_0}|\Delta\chi(r;G,H)|\,dr below the near-duplicate scale r_0, where excess overlap components count distinct images copied.

CIFAR-10 diffusion models at r_0: 132 against 7 on a 200-image memorizer, 13 against 14 on the control. With Pramita Bagchi.

Of 24 generators that copy nothing, a nearest-neighbour statistic rejects 7, T_{\rm small} one; a count at r_0 is as specific. The profile adds steadiness in r_0 and a 3-fold form.

What remains

\chi is signed and blind on annuli. The paper’s relative-homology refinement sees them and is stable; can a test use it directly?

A different scale per cloud: what does the two-parameter profile \Delta\chi(r,s) certify that the diagonal cannot?

Mixup barcodes

arXiv:2608.06180

The mixup barcode of Wagner et al. is the image persistence of \mathcal U(Y;r)\hookrightarrow\mathcal U(X\cup Y;r). It keeps graded homology that \Delta\chi collapses, yet it does not determine \Delta\chi, which needs \chi of one cloud alone. The comparison is by properties.

Timing on the final-epoch representations of 104 networks (arXiv:2609.08561). In fairness: there, total mixup predicts generalization better than the null-normalized quotient, R^2=0.84 against 0.34.

The relative refinement

arXiv:2608.06180

What each cloud adds to the others: \mathrm{RPH}_*(\mathcal X)=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{k}\Big\{H_*\big(\mathcal U(X_i;t_i),\ \mathcal U(X_i;t_i)\cap\textstyle\bigcup_{j\ne i}\mathcal U(X_j;t_j)\big)\Big\}_{\mathbf t\ge0}.

For color-matched perturbations, \epsilon=\max_i d_H(X_i,X_i'), d_I\big(\mathrm{RPH}_*(\mathcal X),\mathrm{RPH}_*(\mathcal X')\big)\le\epsilon in the multiparameter interleaving distance.

Sample Y on a torus and X on a longitude: the overlap is a band and every \chi vanishes, yet H_1(T^2,S^1)=H_2(T^2,S^1)=\mathbb Z. For k\ge3 it is not complete, and it needs persistence rather than a sweep.

High dimensions

arXiv:2609.08561

For an orthogonal projection P and r^\ast(X,Y)=\tfrac12\min_{x\in X,y\in Y}\|x-y\|, r^\ast(PX,PY)\le r^\ast(X,Y): offsets disjoint in the shadow are disjoint in the ambient space.

Measure on a PCA shadow fitted to the pooled clouds, blind to labels, so the tests stay exact: d_0=5, or 4 for triple scans. Depth conclusions are unchanged at d_0=3,4,6: median Spearman 0.91, 0.96, 0.97 against d_0=5.

Concentration of measure. In embeddings of high effective rank the scale window collapses: on copy-detection features (effective rank 574) the topological statistics go null while a nearest-neighbour fraction still fires (arXiv:2610.00251).

The guard in practice

arXiv:2609.08561

Zero firings in 52{,}650 pairwise measurements (42{,}570 distinct clouds), and zero again when 45{,}225 cells were re-evaluated under three forms of the guard. Class overlaps are blobs: \Delta\chi first returns to zero a median 78 of 181 grid points after contact. Zero says the pattern never occurred, not that cancellation is impossible.

X uniform on a disc, Y on a circle inside it: the overlap is an annular band, and the unguarded test certifies separation at p_\downarrow=0.005. The first interaction scale, \hat r^\ast=0.005<0.40, blocks it, at no cost: it falls out of the same sweep.
