The Intersection Euler Characteristic Profile

Euler Calculus, Stability, and Certified Topological Interaction of Point Clouds

16 November 2026

Several clouds, one space

Sightings of two species. Where their ranges overlap, the two can meet.

Single cells of two types. Where the populations blend, one type is turning into the other.

GPS fixes of two vehicles. Their routes cross at two intersections.

Each point carries a ball; in dark red, the overlap. \Delta\chi is its Euler characteristic at the drawn scale.

How much do they interact?

image barcode: chromatic alpha complexes, Cultrera di Montesano et al., arXiv:2212.03128

How much do several point clouds interact topologically, and at what scale?

Far apart. Image persistence is just ordinary persistence of X: Y never fills a feature of X. The overlap is empty until the balls meet.

Touching. Still ordinary persistence of X. The overlap is a lens, \Delta\chi=1, with a blip to 2 where a sliver forms beside it.

Inside the hole. Ordinary persistence of X: its loop lives until 1.40. Image persistence, the same loop seen inside X\cup Y: dead at 0.55, filled by Y. The overlap is the meeting.

One integer per scale: graded homology is lost, and \chi is blind on annuli. Hence the guard of § III, and the paper’s relative refinement.

§ I. Euler Calculus

The profile

arXiv:2608.06180

For disjoint finite clouds \mathcal X=(X_1,\dots,X_k) in \mathbb R^d and scales \mathbf t=(t_1,\dots,t_k)\in\mathbb R^k_{\ge0}, the Euler characteristic of the overlap of the ball unions \mathcal U(X_i;t_i)=\bigcup_{p\in X_i}B(p,t_i): \Delta\chi(\mathbf t;\mathcal X)\;=\;\chi\Big(\bigcap_{i=1}^k\mathcal U(X_i;t_i)\Big),\qquad\text{in the plane: pieces minus holes.}

An Euler integral, \Delta\chi=\int\prod_{i}\mathbf 1_{\mathcal U(X_i;t_i)}\,d\chi; for two clouds on the diagonal \mathbf t=(r,r), \Delta\chi(r)=\chi_X(r)+\chi_Y(r)-\chi_{X\cup Y}(r).

Reading a profile

arXiv:2608.06180

The curve leaves zero at r^\ast, half the gap between the clouds.

At small scales, many tiny lenses where single points meet: the sample, not the shape.

A value held across scales is the answer; Theorem 5 makes it precise.

The clouds meet, yet \chi of an annulus is 0: the blind spot, checked by the guard of § III.

Why this curve

arXiv:2608.06180

Let I(A_1,\dots,A_k)=\Phi(\mathcal X)(\mathbf t) for A_i=\mathcal U(X_i;t_i), defined on finite unions of finite intersections of balls, be

  • a valuation in each cloud: inclusion–exclusion when one cloud’s union is split, the others fixed;
  • homotopy invariant: it depends only on the homotopy type of \bigcap_i A_i;
  • separating: 0 when \bigcap_i A_i=\emptyset;
  • normalized: 1 when \bigcap_i A_i is nonempty and contractible.

Then I(A_1,\dots,A_k)=\chi\big(\bigcap_i A_i\big), that is, \Phi=\Delta\chi. Neither \chi nor an integral is assumed.

It picks which summary of the overlap; it does not say that its value measures strength: \chi is signed and not monotone. Strength is for the tests.

Why not the overlap’s homology?

arXiv:2608.06180

For A=\mathcal U(X;t), B=\mathcal U(Y;s) and \Delta\beta_q=\beta_q(A)+\beta_q(B)-\beta_q(A\cup B), \beta_q(A\cap B)=\Delta\beta_q+\delta_q+\delta_{q+1}, with \delta_q the rank of the connecting map H_q(A\cup B)\to H_{q-1}(A\cap B). The alternating sum cancels the \delta’s: \chi(A\cap B)=\chi(A)+\chi(B)-\chi(A\cup B).

The nerve of the cross-cloud lenses B(x,t)\cap B(y,s) computes the overlap. It has up to mn vertices, and \ell lenses with a common point span 2^\ell-1 faces, with \ell as large as mn.

\Delta\chi is the Euler characteristic curve of the overlap filtration, the alternating sum of its Betti curves, read off the Alpha complexes of the clouds and of their union, never the overlap’s: cheap enough for 999 permutations per test.

§ II. Computation and Stability

Computation

arXiv:2608.06180 · Edelsbrunner, DCG 1995, Thm 3.2 · Edelsbrunner–Mücke, TOG 1994, §3.3

For |X|+|Y|=n in fixed dimension d, sweeping the Alpha complexes of X, Y and X\cup Y in order of entry radius computes \Delta\chi in O\big(n^{\lceil d/2\rceil}\log n\big) time and O\big(n^{\lceil d/2\rceil}\big) space, forming no boundary matrix.

\mathcal U(X;r) has the homotopy type of the Alpha complex at r, and \chi needs no homology: \chi(r)=\sum_{a(\sigma)\le r}(-1)^{\dim\sigma}, with a(\sigma) the radius at which \sigma enters. Measured: 3.7 s per pair, against 31.7 s for the mixup barcode (arXiv:2609.08561).

In even d the exponent is optimal for any algorithm that builds the filtration. k clouds by inclusion–exclusion, O\big(2^k n^{\lceil d/2\rceil}\log n\big); off the diagonal, a weighted Alpha complex.

Stability

arXiv:2608.06180

Let \epsilon=\max_i d_H(X_i,X_i'). Then

  • the overlap filtrations of \mathcal X and \mathcal X' are \epsilon-interleaved;
  • \Delta\chi(r)=\Delta\chi'(r) exactly, whenever [r-\epsilon,r+\epsilon] holds no critical scale (bar endpoint of the overlap) of either;
  • \|\Delta\chi-\Delta\chi'\|_{L^1}\le2\epsilon\,(N+N'), with N,N' the numbers of bars of the two overlap barcodes, all degrees.

\Delta\chi itself admits no interleaving distance, and no L^1 bound free of the bar count can hold. Integer-valued: close becomes equal, away from the critical scales.

§ III. Certified Interaction

Every number carries a test

arXiv:2609.08561

With \Phi(\Delta\chi)=\int|\Delta\chi(r)|\,dr, \widehat E=\frac{\Phi\big(\Delta\chi(\cdot;X_1,\dots,X_k)\big)}{\overline\Phi_B}\,, \overline\Phi_B the mean of \Phi over the test’s B relabelings: about 1 under H_0, tending to 0 under separation.

Under H_0, the labels of the pooled sample exchangeable (the clouds fully mixed), both one-sided permutation tests are exact, \Pr[p\le\alpha]\le\alpha: for \Phi, indeed for any statistic fixed in advance, at every level, sample size and number B of permutations.

A separation claim is withheld when \Delta\chi is silent just after first contact \hat r^\ast, where the unions provably meet. Unguarded, the test certifies an annular overlap as separated, with probability tending to one.

Mixup barcodes

arXiv:2608.06180

The mixup barcode of Wagner et al. is the image persistence of \mathcal U(Y;r)\hookrightarrow\mathcal U(X\cup Y;r). It keeps graded homology that \Delta\chi collapses, yet it does not determine \Delta\chi, which needs \chi of one cloud alone. The comparison is by properties.

mixup barcode \Delta\chi
symmetric in the clouds no yes
k>2 clouds open inclusion–exclusion
hypothesis test open exact permutation tests
cost image persistence, cubic in the simplex count sweep, O(n^{\lceil d/2\rceil}\log n)
measured, identical clouds 31.7 s per pair 3.7 s per pair

Timing on the final-epoch representations of 104 networks (arXiv:2609.08561). In fairness: there, total mixup predicts generalization better than the null-normalized quotient, R^2=0.84 against 0.34.

The relative refinement

arXiv:2608.06180

What each cloud adds to the others: \mathrm{RPH}_*(\mathcal X)=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{k}\Big\{H_*\big(\mathcal U(X_i;t_i),\ \mathcal U(X_i;t_i)\cap\textstyle\bigcup_{j\ne i}\mathcal U(X_j;t_j)\big)\Big\}_{\mathbf t\ge0}.

For color-matched perturbations, \epsilon=\max_i d_H(X_i,X_i'), d_I\big(\mathrm{RPH}_*(\mathcal X),\mathrm{RPH}_*(\mathcal X')\big)\le\epsilon in the multiparameter interleaving distance.

Sample Y on a torus and X on a longitude: the overlap is a band and every \chi vanishes, yet H_1(T^2,S^1)=H_2(T^2,S^1)=\mathbb Z. For k\ge3 it is not complete, and it needs persistence rather than a sweep.

Consistency

arXiv:2608.06180

Let A_1,\dots,A_k\subset\mathbb R^d be compact with reach at least \tau>0, sampled i.i.d. from measures supported on them and positive on balls; let \rho\mapsto T_\rho=\bigcap_i\mathcal U(A_i;\rho) have finitely many critical values in (0,\tau), and r\in(0,\tau) be regular for it. Then almost surely, for all large samples:

  • persistently: with \epsilon_{\max}=\max_i d_H(X_i,A_i)\to0, the \epsilon_{\max}-persistent Euler characteristic of the sample overlap is \chi(T_r);
  • exactly: \Delta\chi(r;X_1,\dots,X_k)=\chi(T_r) when r is also regular for the sample.

O\big(\epsilon^{-k_i}(\log\tfrac1\epsilon+\log\tfrac1\delta)\big) points from A_i for confidence 1-\delta, with \epsilon set by the distance from r to the critical scales: the intrinsic dimension k_i, not the ambient d.

Mostly pairwise

arXiv:2609.08561

For classes c,c',c'' at layer \ell: \ \rho_\ell=\widehat E_\ell(c,c',c'')\,\big/\,\max\big\{\widehat E_\ell(c,c'),\widehat E_\ell(c,c''),\widehat E_\ell(c',c'')\big\}. Only a k-fold profile can pose it.

\rho\le1 in 1{,}509 of 1{,}560 triple–layer cells (96.7\%), median 0.63. An unstructured null already reaches 83\%; the evidence is the medians, 0.44–0.89 against 0.85–1.03 (p=3.3\times10^{-8}). The pairwise matrix carries almost all the class-overlap structure the profile can see: a regularity, not a law.

High dimensions

arXiv:2609.08561

For an orthogonal projection P and r^\ast(X,Y)=\tfrac12\min_{x\in X,y\in Y}\|x-y\|, r^\ast(PX,PY)\le r^\ast(X,Y): offsets disjoint in the shadow are disjoint in the ambient space.

Measure on a PCA shadow fitted to the pooled clouds, blind to labels, so the tests stay exact: d_0=5, or 4 for triple scans. Depth conclusions are unchanged at d_0=3,4,6: median Spearman 0.91, 0.96, 0.97 against d_0=5.

Concentration of measure. In embeddings of high effective rank the scale window collapses: on copy-detection features (effective rank 574) the topological statistics go null while a nearest-neighbour fraction still fires (arXiv:2610.00251).

The guard in practice

arXiv:2609.08561

Zero firings in 52{,}650 pairwise measurements (42{,}570 distinct clouds), and zero again when 45{,}225 cells were re-evaluated under three forms of the guard. Class overlaps are blobs: \Delta\chi first returns to zero a median 78 of 181 grid points after contact. Zero says the pattern never occurred, not that cancellation is impossible.

X uniform on a disc, Y on a circle inside it: the overlap is an annular band, and the unguarded test certifies separation at p_\downarrow=0.005. The first interaction scale, \hat r^\ast=0.005<0.40, blocks it, at no cost: it falls out of the same sweep.

Memorization, counted

arXiv:2610.00251

Does a generator copy its training images? Compare its samples’ overlap with training images T and held-out images H; if it copies nothing, relabelling T and H is an exact null, for a random held-out split.

T_{\rm small}=\int_0^{r_0}|\Delta\chi(r;G,T)|\,dr-\int_0^{r_0}|\Delta\chi(r;G,H)|\,dr below the near-duplicate scale r_0, where excess overlap components estimate the distinct images copied.

CIFAR-10 diffusion models at r_0: 132 against 7 on a 200-image memorizer, 13 against 14 on the control. With Pramita Bagchi.

Of 24 generators that copy nothing, a nearest-neighbour statistic rejects 7, T_{\rm small} one; a count at r_0 is as specific. The profile adds steadiness in r_0 and a 3-fold form.
